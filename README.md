AI Security & Risk Assessment Portfolio

Focus: AI Security, Responsible AI, Risk Assessment and Compliance

About This Project

This portfolio demonstrates practical knowledge of AI security evaluation, risk identification, responsible AI practices, and compliance assessment.

The objective is to examine potential security weaknesses in AI systems, identify risks, and recommend appropriate mitigation strategies.

Areas of Focus

* AI Security Risk Assessment
* Prompt Injection Awareness and Testing
* AI Output Safety and Reliability
* Data Privacy and Protection
* Responsible AI and Policy Compliance
* Risk Identification and Mitigation

Practical Assessment Areas

1. Prompt Injection Risk

Examine how malicious or misleading instructions could manipulate AI system responses.

2. Data Privacy

Assess whether AI systems could expose confidential or sensitive information through inappropriate responses.

3. Output Safety

Evaluate AI-generated responses for harmful, misleading, or policy-violating content.

4. Compliance Assessment

Review AI outputs against defined safety requirements and responsible AI guidelines.

Risk Management Approach

My assessment approach involves:

1. Identifying potential security risks.
2. Evaluating the likelihood and potential impact of each risk.
3. Documenting findings and inconsistencies.
4. Recommending appropriate mitigation measures.
5. Reviewing results against established compliance requirements.

Professional Background

My background in compliance, documentation review, quality assurance, and risk monitoring provides a foundation for evaluating AI systems and supporting responsible AI development.

Project Status

This is an evolving practical learning and portfolio project. Assessment examples and findings will be added as testing activities are completed.
